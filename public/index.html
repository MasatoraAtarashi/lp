What this is

The meal first.
Everything else is built around it.

Most Shibuya guides hand you the same twelve places. We start somewhere else: what do you actually want to eat, and what can you not eat? A resident picks the meal from the counters people here rate highly and they have personally sat at, reserves it, and works backwards from the booking.

The rest of the day goes around that. The famous things too, if you want them — the game floor and the manga basements are a real reason to come, and there is a way to do them that does not cost you three hours in a queue.

Udagawa-cho, Shinsen, Oku-Shibuya, Tomigaya, Yoyogi-Hachiman.

What you receive
